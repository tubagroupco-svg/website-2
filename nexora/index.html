Design my system

The honest part

Most smart homes make you work.

An app for the lights. Another for the lock. Another for the thermostat. You take out your phone, hunt for the right one, wait for it to load, and finally do something a wall switch did in a second.

Nexora removes the hunting. The house acts first, and you get on with your morning.

How it works

Three visits. Then you forget we were here.

01

We walk your home.

One hour, room by room. We map what you already do every day. When you leave, when you sleep, which room is always too cold.

02

We fit it in a day.

Wireless where it can be, wired where it has to be. No channels cut through your walls, no dust sheets for a week.

03

It learns, then it goes quiet.

For two weeks it watches and adjusts. After that you stop noticing it, which is the entire point.
