Why it is fast

Nothing has to leave the room.

Most smart switches send your touch to a server in another country and wait for permission to come back. Nexora decides in the wall. That is the whole trick, and it is why the light is already on before your finger leaves the glass.

  • 01Your touch is read in the panel itself
  • 02The panel switches the circuit directly
  • 03The internet is told afterwards, if it is there

The three things people ask first

Straight answers, before you spend anything.

Do I have to rewire the house?

No. It goes into the switch boxes you already have, in the walls you already have. Most homes are done in a day.

What happens when the wifi drops?

You press it and the light comes on. It is still a switch. The app stops, the wall does not.

Will my family have to learn an app?

No. It is glass you press, in the same place the old switch was. Nobody has to be taught anything.

Questions

The rest of it.

Do I need a neutral wire?
Tell us what is in your board and we will tell you straight. Where a neutral is missing we have a way around it, and we work that out before you pay anything, not after.
Do I need a hub?
No separate hub to buy or hide. The panel is the hub.
Does it work with Alexa and Google?
Yes, both. And it still works when you never open either of them.
What happens in a power cut?
Nothing you would not expect from a switch. When the power comes back, everything is where you left it.
Can I start with one room?
Yes, and most people do. Start with the living room, add the rest whenever you feel like it.
How long does the install take?
A day for most homes. A big house with a lot of points can run into a second day, and we will tell you which one you are before we start.
What does it cost?
It depends on how many points you have, which is why we come and count them. Message us and we will give you a real number, not a range.
