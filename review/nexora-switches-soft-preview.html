How it works

Plain wiring, a different surface.

Underneath the glass is a touch sensor wired into your existing circuit, in the same back box the old rocker switch used. Nothing about the wiring behind it changes — it is still your house’s own live and neutral doing the work, the glass is just how you reach it now. Touch it and the light responds the way a switch always has; the same circuit is also reachable from the app, and stays reachable by touch even if the internet is down.

Questions about this one

What people ask before they book.

Do you have to chase the walls to fit this?
Almost never. It fits the back box that is already there.
What if the wifi goes down?
Touch still works, because you are switching your own wall wiring. What you lose until it is back is the app and voice.
Can I choose what each side controls?
Yes, and what it is called. We set that with you on the visit, not you working it out afterwards.
