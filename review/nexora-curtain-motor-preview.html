How it works

Plain wiring, a different surface.

The motor takes the place of the manual draw on your existing curtain track, driving the same mechanism a hand would. It runs from a scene, a schedule, the remote, or a word to Alexa, grouped into the same scenes your lights and switches already use, so “goodnight” can close the curtains at the same time it turns off the lights. We check your track on the free visit, the same as we check wiring for the rest of the range, because tracks vary and we would rather look than guess.

Where it fits

Not only one kind of room.

Homes

Bedrooms especially — one scene closes the curtains and turns off the lights instead of two separate jobs.

Hotels

Blackout curtains on a schedule, so a room comes up open in the morning without housekeeping doing it by hand.

Commercial spaces

A shop window or a showroom, where the display is meant to open and close on the same schedule every day.

Questions about this one

What people ask before they book.

Will it work with my existing curtains and track?
Usually, but tracks vary enough that we check yours on the free visit rather than promise a fit sight unseen.
How loud is it?
Quiet enough to be worth hearing for yourself on the visit, if that matters for the room it is going in.
Can it open with a scene, not just on its own?
Yes — group it into any scene your lights and switches already use.
