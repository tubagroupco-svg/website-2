How it works

Plain wiring, a different surface.

The dial reads the turn and shows the level back to you on the same glass — a fan speed or a light level, in place of the plastic regulator that did the same job with a click instead of a number. Tap 50% or 100% for the level you use most, or turn it to anywhere between. The two zones below the dial are plain on and off, for when you do not need to think about the level at all. Set from the app too, the same as everything else in the range.

Where it fits

Not only one kind of room.

Homes

Commercial spaces

A restaurant or a salon, where the light level is part of the mood and gets changed more than once a day.

Hotels

A guest room fan or reading light, with the level visible instead of hidden in a switch position nobody remembers.

Questions about this one

What people ask before they book.

Does it work with LED bulbs?
Worth checking on the free visit — it depends on what is already fitted, the same as anything else electrical in an older room.
Is it for fans, lights, or both?
Either — the dial and the two touch zones are set up for whichever is on that circuit.
Can I set the level from my phone?
Yes, the same percentage you would set on the glass.
