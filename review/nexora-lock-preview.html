A bell that reaches you

How it works

Plain wiring, a different surface.

The lock reads a fingerprint or a keypad code at the door and releases the bolt; the camera above it means you can see the door before you answer it. A mechanical key stays as the fallback, the same backup any electronic lock should have, for the day the battery is the last thing on your mind. We check your specific door and its existing hardware on the same free visit we use for everything else in the range, rather than promising a fit before we have seen it.

Where it fits

Not only one kind of room.

Homes

The front door — the one lock in the house that gets used by everyone in it, all day.

Flats and apartments

A door you do not want a spare key hidden under a mat for. Fingerprints and codes can be added or removed as people come and go.

Commercial spaces

A shop or office front door, where knowing who came in and when matters more than it does at home.

Questions about this one

What people ask before they book.

What happens if the battery dies?
The mechanical key stays as the backup, the same as it would with any electronic lock.
Does it fit my existing door?
We check your door and its current hardware on the free visit, the same as we check wiring for everything else.
Can more than one person unlock it?
Yes — fingerprints, codes and app access can be added for everyone in the house and removed just as easily.
