How it works

Plain wiring, a different surface.

It is the same family as the 4 inch panel, built wider for a room that needs more on one plate: several zones, a media tile, and a dedicated thermostat dial rather than sharing the screen with everything else. It goes into a standard wall opening and needs the same neutral wire the 4 inch panel does, checked on the free visit. Whatever you set on the wall is the same thing you would see in the app.

Questions about this one

What people ask before they book.

What is the difference between this and the 4 inch panel?
Room. The wide panel is built for a space with more to control at once — media, several lighting zones, climate. The 4 inch is built for a bedroom or a hallway.
Does it need the same wiring as the small panel?
Yes, a neutral wire, checked on the same free visit.
Can it control more than one room?
It is built to run the room it is mounted in well, and to reach any room from the app, rather than try to be every room’s panel at once.
